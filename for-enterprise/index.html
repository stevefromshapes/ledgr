What Ledgr automates in practice

Working around your existing systems, see what financial workflows Ledgr can automate.

AP Invoice Processing

Read, code and post supplier invoices at scale, with AI handling VAT, account coding and exception flagging.

Supplier Statement Recon

Automated matching against your ledger, with discrepancies surfaced and aged items escalated.

Missing Document Chasing

Agents identify, draft and send chaser emails, and track responses back into your workflow.

Period-End Journals

Prepayments, accruals and month-end journals generated and posted based on your rules.

Finance Communications

Customer and supplier-facing communications handled consistently and at volume.

Automated Billing Workflows

Recurring billing, remittance matching and payment allocation running without manual input.

ERP-Adjacent Automation

Plug the manual gaps that still exist around your ERP without touching the core system.

Finance Query Triage

AI-first handling of inbound finance queries, resolved automatically or routed to the right person.
